## What's new

- **New name and icon.** The app is now spelled **tablex**, with a new icon. The downloads are named `tablex-<version>-macos-<arch>.dmg`. An installed copy updates in place, wherever and however it is named.
- **Smarter SQL completion.**
  - It suggests tables from databases you have not opened in the sidebar yet, and columns of the tables in your statement, aliases included.
  - After `JOIN … ON` it offers the join condition from the foreign keys (`o.customer_id = c.id`).
  - It can expand `SELECT *` into the column list, and adds `()` after a function.
  - Keywords follow your **Keyword case** setting.
  - **⌘I** opens the suggestions by hand.
  - MongoDB scripts get collection and field names.
- **A fuller connection tree.**
  - A table opens into **Columns**, **Indexes**, **Constraints**, **Foreign Keys** and **Triggers**. Each folder loads only when you open it.
  - Drag a table, view, column or collection onto an editor to insert its name, quoted for the database.
  - **Copy Name** and **Copy Qualified Name** (⌘⇧C) work on any object.
  - **F2** renames, **Delete** drops (always through the review dialog), **F4** opens.
  - **Create Database** connects first if needed, and is no longer offered for SQLite and Redis.
  - With **Local search** turned off, searching the sidebar finds tables and collections in databases you have not expanded.
- **Missing drivers are one click away.**
  - The download mark on a database in **New Connection** installs its driver, with progress.
  - **Next**, **Test** or **Save & Connect** with a database whose driver is missing asks to download it, then carries on.
  - If the driver list is still loading, tablex waits for it instead of failing. When offline, it says so and offers **Retry**.
- **Agents can run migrations.** For the AI assistant and tablex-mcp, a change that only adds (`CREATE TABLE`, `CREATE INDEX`, `ALTER TABLE … ADD COLUMN`, `COMMENT ON` …) is now an ordinary change: on a **Read and write** connection you approve it once and it runs. A statement that can destroy data (`DROP`, `TRUNCATE`, `ALTER … DROP/ALTER COLUMN`, `RENAME`, `DELETE` or `UPDATE` without `WHERE` …) still needs your explicit approval with its risk spelled out, and through MCP it runs only on a connection that is not of a production type.
- **Place the Error Log** at the bottom, on the right, or in a tab of its own. tablex remembers your choice.
- **Vim keys** show a block cursor in Normal and Visual mode.
- An empty result area has a **Run** button, or **Load** for tables, collections and Redis keys.
- The filter buttons are clearer: **Remove filter** and **Start over**, with tooltips. A divider separates them from the filter. The filter button no longer turns grey while a filter is applied.

## Fixes

- **Keychain prompts after updates** ([#1](https://github.com/tablex-org/tablex/issues/1)). Saved passwords are now kept encrypted in the app's database, under a single key in the macOS Keychain. tablex moves your existing passwords there once.
  - After a tablex-mcp install or update, **Driver Manager** asks for Keychain access once. Choose **Always Allow**.
  - Choosing **Deny** stops at once, instead of asking for every connection.
- Choosing a keyword such as `IN` from the suggestions inserted it in lowercase.
- Remaining English labels in the sidebar are now translated.
